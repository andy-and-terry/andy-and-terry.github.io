Misc Toolkit

7 browser-based utilities. Everything runs locally in your tab — no uploads, no accounts, no server. Pick a tool to get started.

🔳
QR Code
Generate a QR code from text or a URL and download it as a PNG.
 📷
QR Scanner
Scan a QR code from an uploaded image or your webcam.
 📊
Barcode
Generate Code128, EAN, UPC, and Code39 barcodes as PNG.
 🎨
Color Tools
Pick colors, generate palettes, convert formats, check contrast.
 📏
Unit Converter
Live conversion for length, weight, temperature, and data size.
 🔑
Password Generator
Generate strong random passwords, one at a time or in bulk.
 🕒
Timestamp
Convert between Unix timestamps and human-readable dates.
Runs locally in your browser. No files leave your device.
