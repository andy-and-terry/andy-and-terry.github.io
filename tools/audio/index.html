Audio Toolkit

6 browser-based audio tools. Everything runs locally in your tab via ffmpeg.wasm — no uploads, no accounts, no server. Pick a tool to get started.

✂️
Trim / Convert
Cut a clip down and export as MP3, WAV, OGG, AAC, or FLAC.
 📶
Normalize
Even out loudness to a target LUFS level.
 🔇
Silence Remove
Auto-trim leading, trailing, and internal silence.
 🔗
Merge
Join multiple clips into one, in order.
 ⏩
Pitch / Speed
Change playback speed and pitch independently.
 🌊
Waveform Video
Render a waveform visualization as an MP4.
Runs locally via ffmpeg.wasm. No files leave your device.
