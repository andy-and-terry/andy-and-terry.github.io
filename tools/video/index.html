🎬 Video Toolkit

Video Toolkit

16 browser-based video tools. Everything runs locally in your tab via ffmpeg.wasm — no uploads, no accounts, no server. Pick a tool to get started.

✂️
Trim
Cut a clip down to a start and end point.
 🗜️
Compress
Shrink file size with quality and resolution controls.
 🔗
Merge
Join multiple clips into one, in order.
 ⏩
Speed
Speed up or slow down playback, with pitch-correct audio.
 🔄
Rotate / Flip
Fix sideways footage or mirror the frame.
 📸
Extract Frame
Grab a full-quality still image from any moment.
 🎵
Audio
Pull the audio track out as MP3, WAV, M4A, or OGG.
 🔲
Crop
Drag to select and crop to a region of the frame.
 🏷️
Watermark
Overlay a logo image or text, anywhere on the frame.
 ⏪
Reverse
Play a clip backwards, audio included.
 💬
Subtitles
Burn an .srt or .vtt file into the video permanently.
 🎨
Color
Adjust brightness, contrast, saturation, and gamma.
 🪃
Boomerang
Forward-then-reverse loop, like the classic effect.
 🖼️
Slideshow
Turn a set of photos into a video with music.
 ✨
Denoise
Smooth out grain and compression noise.
 🔳
Thumbnail Grid
Generate a contact-sheet image of evenly spaced frames.
Runs locally via ffmpeg.wasm. No files leave your device.
