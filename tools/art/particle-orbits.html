🧬 Art Toolkit

Particle Orbits

Particles swing around a small system of gravity wells, tracing elliptical trails. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing uploads anywhere.

Seed

Same seed and parameters always reproduce the same artwork.

Parameters

Strength of pull toward each attractor.
Initial tangential velocity of each particle.
Lower values leave longer, more persistent trails.

Export

Runs locally via p5.js. No files leave your device.
