Voronoi Cells

Random seed points tessellate the canvas into cells of nearest-neighbor territory, optionally relaxed toward an even, soap-bubble arrangement. Runs entirely in your browser.

Seed

Same seed and parameters always reproduce the same artwork.

Parameters

Iteratively moves each seed toward its cell's centroid for a more even, organic tiling. 0 keeps the raw random scatter.
Grid cell size used to approximate the diagram. Lower is crisper but slower to compute.

Export

Runs locally via p5.js. No files leave your device.
