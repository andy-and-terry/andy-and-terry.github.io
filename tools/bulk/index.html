📦 Bulk Toolkit

Bulk Toolkit

Batch-run the video and art tools across many files at once. Everything still runs locally in your tab — no uploads, no accounts, no server.

🎬
Video Bulk
Apply one video operation to a whole batch of files, one after another.
 🧬
Art Bulk
Generate a grid of algorithmic art variations from randomized seeds.
Runs locally via ffmpeg.wasm and p5.js. No files leave your device.
