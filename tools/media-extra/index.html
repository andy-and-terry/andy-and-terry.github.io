🧪 Media Extras

Media Extras

A few extra browser-based media tools that don't fit the Video Toolkit. Everything runs locally via ffmpeg.wasm and the browser's own media APIs — no uploads, no accounts, no server.

🎞️
GIF Maker
Turn a video clip or a stack of images into an animated GIF.
 🎵
Video to Audio
Extract the audio track as MP3, WAV, or AAC.
 🖥️
Screen Recorder
Record your screen or a browser tab and download the video.
 ⚖️
Compress Compare
See a video at several compression levels side by side.
Runs locally via ffmpeg.wasm. No files leave your device.
