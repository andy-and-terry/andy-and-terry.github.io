🧪 Media Extras

Screen Recorder

Record your screen, a window, or a browser tab directly in the browser and download the result. Nothing is uploaded — recording happens locally via the MediaRecorder API.

Your browser doesn't support screen recording (the getDisplayMedia or MediaRecorder API is missing). Try a recent desktop version of Chrome, Firefox, or Edge — most mobile browsers don't support this yet.

1. Options

MP4 recording depends on your browser's built-in encoder support. If it's unavailable, this falls back to WebM automatically.
